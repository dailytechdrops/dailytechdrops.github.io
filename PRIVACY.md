# Privacy Policy for Daily Tech Drops

Daily Tech Drops ("we") runs the Instagram account @daily_tech_drops and a Facebook Page of the same name. This policy covers the small tool ("Daily Tech Drops Poster") we use to publish our own posts and answer comments on our own accounts.

## What we collect
- The username and comment text of people who comment on our posts, so we can reply with a product link.
- Nothing else. We do not collect names, emails, phone numbers, payment details, or location.

## How we use it
- Only to send the person who commented a reply that contains the product link they asked for.
- We do not sell, rent, or share this information with anyone.

## How long we keep it
- A comment identifier is kept in our own records so we never send the same reply twice. We do not keep message content about you beyond that.

## Affiliate disclosure
As an Amazon Associate we earn from qualifying purchases. Links we send are Amazon affiliate links.

## Your choices and deletion
To have anything we hold about you deleted, message @daily_tech_drops on Instagram or email the address listed on our Instagram profile. See [DATA_DELETION.md](DATA_DELETION.md).

## Changes
We may update this policy. The latest version is always at this address.
