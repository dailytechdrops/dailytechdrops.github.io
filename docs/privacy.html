Privacy policy

Daily Tech Drops runs the Instagram account @daily_tech_drops, the Facebook Page and the YouTube channel of the same name. This policy covers the small tool ("Daily Tech Drops Poster") we use to publish our own posts and answer comments on our own accounts.

What we collect

The username and comment text of people who comment on our Instagram posts, so we can reply with the product link they asked for. Nothing else: no names, emails, phone numbers, payment details or location.

YouTube

The tool uses the YouTube Data API only to upload our own videos to our own channel. It does not read, store or share any YouTube viewer data. It uses YouTube API Services, so the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy also apply. You can revoke the tool's access at any time at Google security settings.

How we use it

Only to send the person who commented the link they asked for. We do not sell, rent or share this information.

How long we keep it

A comment identifier is kept so we never send the same reply twice. Nothing else about you is kept.

Affiliate disclosure

As an Amazon Associate we earn from qualifying purchases. Links we share are Amazon affiliate links.

Deletion

To have anything we hold about you deleted, message @daily_tech_drops on Instagram.
